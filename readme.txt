VIDEO SWITCHER
==============

FIRST TIME ONLY
---------------
1. If you're looking at this inside the zip file: close it, right-click the
   zip, choose "Extract All...", and click Extract. Use the new folder.

2. Double-click "Start Video Switcher".

3. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC".
   That's normal for files from the internet. Click "More info",
   then click "Run anyway". (You only have to do this once.)

4. If Windows asks whether to allow access on networks, you can click
   Cancel / Don't allow. Video Switcher doesn't need the internet.


EVERY TIME
----------
1. Double-click "Start Video Switcher".
   A black window opens, then your web browser opens the video screen.
2. Press F11 for full screen (press F11 again to leave full screen).
3. Press H to show or hide the menu, where you can pick a loadout
   or open Setup.

To stop: close the black window.
Don't close the black window while you're using Video Switcher.


ADDING VIDEOS
-------------
Put video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov, .m4v) in the "videos" folder,
then go to Setup to assign them to keys.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- Browser didn't open? Look in the black window: it shows a web
  address (like http://127.0.0.1:8000/index.html). Type that into
  your browser.
- Webcam not showing? When the browser asks to use your camera,
  click Allow.
- Still stuck? Send the file "server.log" from this folder to
  whoever gave you Video Switcher.
